# References for the Thaumoctopus mimicus research files

Research date: 2026-11-13. Covers every source cited in `species-overview.md` and `mimicry-evidence.md`.

Access levels:

- **Full text** = I read the complete paper or page.
- **Abstract only** = I read the abstract and bibliographic record, not the body.
- **Secondary** = I did not open the source itself; what is reported comes from another source that describes it (named in the note).
- **Database** = a database record.

No page numbers, DOIs or quotations in the research files were reconstructed from memory; each was taken from the source or record named here.

## E. Search coverage for the "no peer-reviewed source found" statements

For stingray and snake eel (targets 04 and 05), the following were checked and contained no mention: full text of [Norman2001]; full text of [Huffard2010]; abstracts of [Hanlon2008], [NormanHochberg2005] and [Hanlon2010]; [AMNH2015]; PubMed (search on *Thaumoctopus*, "mimic octopus", octopus flatfish mimicry — the only *T. mimicus* behaviour paper indexed there is [Norman2001]); and several web searches combining the species name with "stingray" and "snake eel", which returned only popular lists without primary citations. This is evidence of absence from the accessible literature, not proof that no such report exists in a book or in the unread body of the paywalled papers.
