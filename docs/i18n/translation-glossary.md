# Translation glossary

Use these terms everywhere. If you change one, change every occurrence in that locale.

Status: chosen by the translating model from general zoological usage. **No native-speaking biologist has reviewed them.** Terms marked † are descriptive coinages or less-standard choices and are the first candidates for review.

## Never translated

- Brand: `Mimic Lab` / `MIMIC LAB`.
- Latin names: *Thaumoctopus mimicus*, *Octopus vulgaris*, *Wunderpus photogenicus*, *Zebrias*, *Pterois*, *Laticauda*, *Megalactis*, *Bothus*. They live in data (`scientificName`) or appear verbatim in text; a test checks they survive translation.
- Arm codes `L1`–`R4`, reference titles, authors, journals, DOIs, URLs, licence and product names.
